About BIAS

Birla Institute of Applied Sciences (BIAS) Bhimtal is a premier engineering institute located in the serene environs of Bhimtal, Uttarakhand. Established in 1969, it is committed to excellence in technical education and research, fostering innovation and leadership among students.
