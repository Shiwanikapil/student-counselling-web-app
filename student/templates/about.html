About - Birla Institute

Welcome to Birla Institute of Applied Sciences

About Our College

Birla Institute of Applied Sciences (BIAS), located in the scenic town of Bhimtal, is one of the premier institutions offering quality education in Engineering and Applied Sciences. The institute is affiliated with Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Technical University and is known for its excellent academic environment, qualified faculty, and modern infrastructure.

At BIAS, we aim to nurture innovation, research, and skill development to prepare students for successful careers in the tech industry.

History of BIAS

BIAS was established in the early 2000s under the aegis of the Birla Group, with the mission to provide affordable and quality technical education in the hills of Uttarakhand. Over the years, the institute has grown to become a reputed name in Northern India for engineering and applied sciences education.

Courses Offered

  • B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering
  • B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering
  • BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)
  • MCA (Master of Computer Applications)

Infrastructure

The campus is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories, smart classrooms, high-speed internet, and a digital library. The lush green environment and peaceful surroundings make it an ideal place for learning and personal growth.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a center of excellence in technical education and innovation.
Mission: To foster a learning ecosystem that encourages creativity, collaboration, and commitment toward nation-building.

Placement & Industry Collaboration

BIAS has a dedicated Training and Placement Cell that organizes campus drives, industrial visits, and internships. Leading companies like TCS, Wipro, Infosys, Tech Mahindra, and startups from NCR and Bangalore hire from our campus.
